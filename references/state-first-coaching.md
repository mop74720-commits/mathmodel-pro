# State-first Coaching

## Why

A real competition does not move as one global pipeline. Q1 may already have a Final Run while Q2 is still ambiguous and Q3 paper text is being drafted. A global stage field hides this reality.

## Minimal decision view

For each active question, identify only what materially affects the next decision:

- what is known;
- what is uncertain;
- what evidence exists;
- what is blocked;
- what decision would change if one more test were run.

Then rank candidate actions by impact, uncertainty, dependency, cost and reversibility. Do not convert this into a ceremonial score unless there is a real decision benefit.

## Stop conditions

Exploration should stop when additional work is unlikely to change the chosen model/decision enough to justify its cost, or when preserving a coherent, reproducible submission has higher expected value.

## Recovery

When new evidence invalidates upstream assumptions, repair the smallest affected layer first. Do not restart the whole workflow unless the factual baseline itself is wrong.
